Subject: SIVP submission rejected as out of scope — SIU 2026 extended paper

Dear Prof. Çetin,

Following your invitation to SIU 2026 authors, we submitted our extended
manuscript to Signal, Image and Video Processing:

  Title:         Confinement-loss prediction in photonic crystal fiber SPR
                 sensors: kernel surrogates versus generative data augmentation
  Authors:       Mohammad Khaleel Hammoud, Cem Kalyoncu, Ahmet Yaşlı
                 (European University of Lefke)
  Submission ID: 7d8d9e69-85d5-438a-b7a3-36e8c102db70
  SIU paper:     doi:10.1109/SIU71813.2026.11636469

The manuscript passed the technical check and was rejected before review by
the editorial office, on the grounds that it is not within the scope of the
journal.

Since we submitted at your invitation, I wanted to bring this to your
attention in case the decision was made without that context. We have not
accepted the transfer offer, so the submission is still with SIVP.

The work is a machine-learning study: it compares kernel regression against
generative data augmentation for surrogate modelling, and reports an
explainability analysis, a Monte Carlo robustness study and measured
training and inference costs. The application is a photonic sensor, but the
contribution is methodological.

If you would like me to resubmit, reframe the manuscript, or take it
elsewhere, I would be grateful for your guidance.

With thanks for your time,
